Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Spice Fresh account and associated data.

How to request deletion

To delete your account, send a deletion request to the email below from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

spiceplanetadmin@gmail.com

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, phone number, email)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Order history
  • Any other account-linked data we hold

Your account is not deactivated or paused — it is permanently deleted, along with the associated data listed above.

Processing time

Deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been removed.

Note: Some information may be retained where required by law (for example, transaction records for tax or fraud-prevention purposes) even after account deletion.
