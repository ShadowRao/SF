Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Open the app and go to Order History to see the status of your current and past orders.

How do I change my delivery address?

Go to your Address settings from the account menu, where you can add, edit, or set a default delivery address.

My payment didn't go through. What do I do?

Check your Order History for the affected order and tap "Retry Payment." If the issue continues, email us with your order number and we'll help sort it out.

How do I request a refund or report a problem with my order?

Email us at spiceplanetadmin@gmail.com with your order number and a description of the issue, and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at spiceplanetadmin@gmail.com with your account details and we'll process your request in line with our Privacy Policy.
